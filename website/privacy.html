Festival data

The app downloads festival data from the official website of the "Rudolstadt Festival", including program information, artist details, locations, maps, and news.

Your festival profile and account sync

Saved events, artist ratings, badge settings, and artist notes are stored on your device. You can use these features without an account. If you sign in with Apple, Google, an email link, or a secret account key, Firebase Authentication manages your account and the app sends your profile, including private notes, to the Firebase backend to sync your own devices. The account identifier separates your private profile from other users. Private note versions are retained for conflict recovery. Firestore data and profile-processing functions use the Frankfurt region. The data is not used for advertising or tracking. See Google’s privacy policy.

Secret account keys are a separate sign-in option for standalone accounts without an email address. They cannot be added to Apple, Google or email accounts. The backend stores a hash of the key linked to your account, rather than the key itself. Anyone with the key can access the account and its private notes. Replacing a key prevents new sign-ins with the previous key; devices already signed in stay signed in. Request limits use hashed IP addresses or account identifiers with counters to reduce abuse. The hosting infrastructure also processes connection information when handling requests.

The current app imports existing local profile data and keeps a local backup. It does not access previous CloudKit data or change previous CloudKit shares. Earlier app versions may still use those shares.

You can delete your account in the app’s Account screen. This removes your Firebase account, synced profiles and private note history, shared profiles, friendships, invitations and account keys. Your current profile and unsent changes remain on the device for local use. Apple accounts require confirming the Apple sign-in again so its authorization can be revoked. A minimal deletion job containing the account identifier, hashed deletion receipts and processing metadata blocks old sessions and retries interrupted cleanup. It is removed automatically after a two-hour protection window and the next successful cleanup run. Shared abuse-prevention counters without account identifiers are separate from profile data.

Sharing with friends

Friendships apply across festivals and years. Each person independently chooses, per friend, whether to share saved events, artist ratings and current artist notes through Firebase. These choices apply to all festival editions; your name and profile color identify you to friends. Notes are private by default. Enabling note sharing gives that friend access to current note text across all festival editions. Note version history always remains restricted to your own account. Changing a friend’s permissions removes further access to the affected data categories across festivals. Ending a friendship removes further access to shared profiles; a device that is offline may keep a previously downloaded copy until it reconnects.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.
